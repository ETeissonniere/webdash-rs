# Home Services

A monorepo of streamlined homelab++ services. Services are coded in Rust and follow the below principles:
1. You Ain't Gonna Need It: while plenty of competing services may come feature packed, we specifically remove anything that may not be massively usable/desired so as to keep things small and snappy
2. Tight: each service is tightly contained, statically compiled, and shipped within the smallest docker container possible

We aim for a high quality codebase with opinionated, high impact, services to cover most homelab usage.
